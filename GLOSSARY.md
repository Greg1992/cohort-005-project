# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and can ask for their money back. This glossary fixes the language for purchases and refunds.

## Language

### Purchases

**Learner**:
A person who has bought or enrolled in a course.
_Avoid_: Student, customer, buyer

**Purchase**:
One learner buying one course, at the price they paid.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of several Seats in one course, made for a team.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One learner's place in a course, issued from a Team Licence.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat that a team member has taken. It belongs to the team, so that learner cannot refund it. Refunding a Team Licence or its unclaimed Seats is not yet defined.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase. An admin decides it by hand.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, pending chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner once an admin approves their Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
How much a Refund returns. It is always the amount the learner paid for the Purchase, never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, price

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase in which a Refund Request can be raised. It includes the day of purchase and the 14th day after it.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can still have a Refund Request. It needs to be inside the Refund Window, with less than 2 hours watched, less than half the lessons complete, and not a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Requested**:
A Refund Request that an admin has not decided yet. Learners see it as "awaiting review".
_Avoid_: Pending, Open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request an admin has accepted. It results in a Refund and a Cancelled Purchase.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request an admin has turned down. The learner gets no money back and keeps access. A Decline is final: the Purchase cannot have another Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Cancelled Purchase**:
A Purchase whose Refund Request was Approved. The learner no longer has access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversed purchase

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the learner raises with their card issuer, outside Cadence, that pulls the payment back. It is not a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Using this word for anything a learner asks Cadence for
